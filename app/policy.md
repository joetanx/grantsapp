# Fictional Community Support Grant — illustrative policy

Version: 1.0

This program and its criteria are entirely fictional, for a synthetic-data demonstration only. They are not government policy, and cannot establish eligibility or guarantee a grant.

The sample intake asks for applicant name, contact email, household size (a positive whole number), monthly household income (a nonnegative number, including zero), and a short description of financial need. A citizen creates an application, saves information, and submits a complete application for an advisory assessment. A status of submitted can mean information is still needed. Staff may ask for clarification or make the final human decision. Citizens may drop an active application. This demo does not determine a benefit amount or provide payments.
