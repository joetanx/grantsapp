# Illustrative fictional assessment rubric

Version: 1.0

This rubric is fictional and advisory only. It cannot establish real eligibility or predict or make a decision. A human staff member makes the final decision.

- R1 — Intake completeness: report whether the five sample fields are present. Server validation independently determines completeness.
- R2 — Household circumstances: summarize the stated household size and income without inferring an income threshold or inventing eligibility rules.
- R3 — Financial need: summarize the stated need and note any uncertainty or ambiguity; do not infer facts.
- R4 — Policy limitations: identify that the fictional policy does not supply an income threshold, award amount, or real eligibility rules.
